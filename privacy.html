About this notice

ōshiro is an offline Sudoku game with automatic castle growth, published under Krēˈādiv Worx. This draft describes the offline edition prepared for Huawei AppGallery, based on candidate v0.44.0. Deen Storkey is responsible for the information described here; Krēˈādiv Worx is a personal brand and is not a registered company.

You do not need a game account to play. The offline game does not send your progress, puzzle activity or device identifiers to us. It has no in-game advertising, analytics, payment or remote crash-reporting service. Downloading or updating through an app store is separate from playing the installed game.

Information kept on your device

The game stores your puzzle boards and entries, pencil notes, locked numbers, move history, puzzle times, results, earned level, XP and castle progress. It also keeps preferences, lesson completion and local recovery copies. These records let you resume puzzles, restore a previous save and retain earned growth.

This information stays in the app’s local storage unless you choose to export and share a copy. Local progress has no automatic game cloud sync. We cannot recover a save that you have not kept or chosen to share.

Optional local playtest log

The playtest log is off by default. If you turn it on in Help, it keeps daily totals on this device: game opens, number entries, completed and assisted puzzles, solve time, earned XP, save errors and completion counts by puzzle mode. It uses your device’s local dates and does not record a raw input replay, name or account identifier.

It keeps up to 180 active dates, removing the oldest date when that limit is exceeded. Turning logging off stops new records but keeps existing ones. Clear playtest log in Help removes that log and stops recording without deleting your game progress. Export playtest log creates a copy only when you request it; the game does not automatically send that copy to us.

Importing, exporting and sharing

Import progress reads the JSON file you select using Android’s document picker. Export progress and Export previous save write a JSON copy to a destination you choose. Progress exports contain saved puzzle and progression data plus an export date; they do not automatically include the separate preferences, lesson state or playtest log.

Your chosen document provider may be a cloud drive. That provider may upload or handle your file under its own settings and privacy terms, even though this offline game has no Internet permission. Anyone you share a save or log with can read its contents. Keep exported files in a place you trust and delete unwanted copies separately.

Permissions, sound and other services

The inspected offline candidate requests no Android permissions. It does not request your contacts, camera, microphone or location, and does not use an advertising identifier. Import and export use the file you choose through the system picker instead of broad storage access.

Optional encouragement plays two prerecorded sounds bundled with the game. It starts off, does not listen to you and uses no speech recording or online voice service.

Android, your WebView provider, app store and document providers have their own services and privacy terms. This notice describes the game’s handling of information; it does not claim that your whole device or those providers never communicate online.

Keeping and deleting local information

Game progress, preferences and recovery copies stay locally until replaced through normal play or removed with the app’s data. Reset the puzzle clears the active attempt after confirmation; it does not delete all progress, earned XP or castle growth.

To remove the app’s locally stored information, use Android Settings to clear this app’s storage/data, or uninstall it. Menu names vary by device. This removes local saves and recovery copies. Files you exported elsewhere remain until you delete them from those locations, including any cloud-provider copies.

The app disables Android’s automatic backup and declares exclusions for cloud backup and device transfer. Manufacturer migration tools may behave differently. Export a progress file before changing phones or uninstalling if you want to keep your game. Earned castle growth does not decay during play, but clearing storage or uninstalling can remove the local record.

Protecting your information

App-private information relies on Android’s app sandbox and your device’s protection. Saves and exported files are readable JSON; the game does not add its own encryption. Protect your device and handle exports with care.

Support, privacy requests and children

Who is responsible

Deen Storkey, operating under the personal brand Krēˈādiv Worx, South Africa, is responsible for this game and support correspondence. The brand is not a registered company. Public correspondence address: pending confirmation. For support or privacy requests, email kreadivworx@proton.me. Deen is the only person managing the studio’s support inbox; email and hosting providers process information as described below.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the sender address, message, email metadata and attachments you choose to send. Providing this information is voluntary. You can play offline without contacting us. Without a reply address or enough detail, we may be unable to answer or investigate the issue. Device and game version can help; a complete save is not required for every request. Please leave out passwords, identity documents and unrelated personal information.

We use correspondence to answer your request, troubleshoot the issue and handle privacy requests. We do not use support messages for advertising or sell them. Where applicable, the basis is our legitimate interest in providing requested support and protecting the service, and compliance with legal duties when handling a rights request or required disclosure. Sending an email does not give permission for unrelated marketing. If a further use needs consent, we will ask separately. We do not make automated decisions about people or profile them through the support inbox.

Support retention

We delete support correspondence and supplied attachments from the inbox and any working copies under our control within 90 days after the request is resolved. Unnecessary attachments may be deleted sooner. We keep information longer only where a legal obligation or a specific legal claim requires it, limit that retained copy to the required purpose, and delete it when that reason ends. The 90-day rule applies to support correspondence, not your on-device game save. Provider backup retention is separate and is described by the providers below.

Email providers and international handling

Support email is handled through Proton Mail, provided by Proton AG in Switzerland. Proton states that Mail servers are in Switzerland, Germany or Norway and encrypted backups are kept for up to 30 days. Proton can process email metadata; ordinary email exchanged with an external provider is not necessarily end-to-end encrypted. Your own email provider also handles your message. See Proton’s general privacy policy for its handling. Deen accesses correspondence from South Africa, so support information may be processed outside your country. The website host’s international handling is described below. Applicable transfer requirements and regional disclosures remain part of the pre-release review; this draft is not a claim of worldwide compliance.

Your privacy requests

You may contact Deen to ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access or correction, request deletion or restriction where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where consent is the basis, you may withdraw it for future processing. Portability and other rights may apply under your local law. These rights have legal conditions and exceptions. We respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful refusal or permitted extension. We may ask only for information reasonably needed to verify the requester; do not send an identity document unless specifically necessary and a suitable method has been agreed.

We cannot remotely read or delete your device’s game save. See “Keeping and deleting local information” for device controls. You can separately request deletion of a save or log you have emailed to us. You may complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator; its contact page lists POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za and 0800 017 160. Where applicable, you can also complain to the data-protection authority in your country.

Children and families

ōshiro is intended for players of all ages, including children. This describes the intended audience; the AppGallery content rating is still pending. Offline play does not require a name, age, email address or account, and the game does not send us player activity. There is no in-game chat, advertising or player-to-player messaging.

A parent or guardian should handle support, privacy requests and sharing exports for a child, using the adult’s contact details and leaving out the child’s identifying information. If we become aware that unnecessary personal information about a child has been sent, we will remove it promptly. If a request genuinely requires a child’s personal information, we will establish the necessary parent/guardian authority and lawful basis before further processing. A parent or guardian can contact Deen to request access to or deletion of information we hold about their child, subject to appropriate verification.
