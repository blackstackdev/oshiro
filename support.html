How do I keep my progress when changing phones?

In Help, choose Export progress and save the JSON file somewhere you trust. On the other phone, choose Import progress and review the selected save. There is no automatic game cloud sync.

Import restores the selected boards and records while keeping earned XP and castle growth from both saves. Preferences, lesson state and the optional playtest log are separate from a progress export. Keep the original file until you have checked the imported game.

If a recent save seems wrong, check the game’s previous-save or recovery options before clearing app storage or uninstalling. An export you did not keep cannot be recovered by this website.

What does locking a number do?

Select a number you entered, then choose Lock. Undo, Redo, Erase and ordinary edits preserve it until you deliberately unlock it. A lock records your confidence; it does not check whether the number is correct.

Pencil notes help you track possibilities. Hints and Check puzzle are available when you want guidance. Your earned level and the puzzle’s difficulty are different: fresh Practice starts at Novice, while a saved puzzle resumes its own difficulty.

Does Reset remove my castle?

No. Reset clears the current puzzle’s entries, notes, locks, timer and Undo/Redo totals after confirmation, and starts a Repeat attempt. Earned XP, castle growth, completed records and other saved puzzles stay.

Clearing the app’s storage/data in Android Settings or uninstalling is different: it removes the local game and recovery data. Export first if you want to keep your progress. Exported files remain where you saved them until you delete those copies.

How do I turn on encouragement?

Open Preferences and enable voice encouragement. It starts off and plays two short prerecorded clips locally. There is no microphone recording or online speech service.

Encouragement checks entries against the solution and counts as help used. It does not reduce the 100 XP earned for a new starting grid. You can turn it off again in Preferences.
